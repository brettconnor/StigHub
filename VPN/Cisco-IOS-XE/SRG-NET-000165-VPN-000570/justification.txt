For Cisco IOS-XE VPN routers running approved versions, Cisco's compliance letters identify IC2M #5356 and CiscoSSL FIPS Provider #5430 for applicable services including IPsec; NIST lists both as active FIPS 140-3 validated modules when operated in approved mode. The dedicated P-256 EC private key is generated on the router without the exportable option, bound to the VPN-TP trustpoint and IKEv2 profiles, and managed under the approved console-only local privileged-access policy.
