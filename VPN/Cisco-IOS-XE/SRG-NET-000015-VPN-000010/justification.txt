The operator-defined WAN ingress policy authorizes only VPN transport from the designated peer; ordinary WAN IP traffic is not authorized. The WAN-VPN-IN and WAN-VPN6-IN access lists implement this policy by permitting only IKE on UDP destination port 500 and native ESP from the configured peer address to the router's WAN address. 

This fix supports the requirement by enforcing the stated WAN ingress authorization boundary.
