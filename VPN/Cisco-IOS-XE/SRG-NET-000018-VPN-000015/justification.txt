The operator-defined information flow control policy authorizes only peer-specific IKE and native ESP at the WAN ingress boundary. The WAN-VPN-IN and WAN-VPN6-IN access lists enforce this policy by restricting inbound IPv4 and IPv6 flows to UDP destination port 500 and ESP from the designated peer address to the router's WAN address.

Applying these access lists inbound on the WAN Layer 3 interface enforces the authorized sources, destinations, and protocols at the network boundary, supporting the requirement to control information flow according to organization-defined policy.
