The IKEv2 proposal uses SHA-256 for its PRF/KDF, and AES-GCM-256 provides authenticated integrity for the site-to-site ESP tunnels without a separate HMAC. For VPN-capable IOS-XE routers on approved releases, the matching Cisco compliance letters map IPsec to FIPS-validated modules #5356 and #5430; their approved-mode algorithms include SHA-2, IKEv2 KDFs, and random-bit generation. The optional X.509 template uses SHA-256 for enrollment and requires SHA-256-or-stronger certificate signatures.
