Under the operator-established console-management scope for this SRG, SSH/VTY, NETCONF, HTTP/HTTPS, and RESTCONF are unnecessary remote-management entry points for the site-to-site VPN router. Disabling the web and API services and denying inbound transports on every VTY line prohibits these unnecessary management paths while retaining local console administration and the approved IKEv2/IPsec VPN role.
