On a platform and IOS XE release covered by its matching Cisco FIPS compliance letter, the router uses cryptographic modules validated by NIST CMVP for IPsec services. The PSK baseline selects AES-GCM-256, SHA-256 PRF, group 19, and peer-specific pre-shared-key authentication for IKEv2, then binds those profiles to AES-GCM IPsec. These settings use the validated modules' approved cryptographic services for site-to-site VPN authentication and protection.
