
SRG-NET-000169-VPN-000610

Severity:
medium

SRG Requirement:
The VPN Gateway must uniquely identify and authenticate non-organizational users (or processes acting on behalf of non-organizational users).

SRG VulDiscussion:
Lack of authentication and identification enables non-organizational users to gain access to the network or possibly a VPN gateway that provides opportunity for intruders to compromise resources within the network infrastructure.

This requirement only applies to components where this is specific to the function of the device or has the concept of a non-organizational user.

SRG Check:
Configure the VPN Gateway to uniquely identify and authenticate non-organizational users (or processes acting on behalf of non-organizational users).

If the VPN Gateway does not uniquely identify and authenticate non-organizational users (or processes acting on behalf of non-organizational users), this is a finding.

SRG Fix:
Configure the VPN Gateway to uniquely identify and authenticate non-organizational users (or processes acting on behalf of non-organizational users).
